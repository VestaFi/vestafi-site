## What Information We Collect

We collect personal and credit information required to provide lending and financial services. This may include:

- Your name, contact details, date of birth, and identification documents
- Loan application details and credit-related data from lenders or credit reporting bodies
- Any details submitted via pre-assessment or enquiry forms (via Fillout)
- Information you provide when you use our mobile app, calculators, or client portal

We do not collect or store tax file numbers, payslips, or trust deeds unless legally required.

## How We Collect Information

We may collect information:

- Directly from you (forms, calls, meetings, email, or online tools)
- Through smart form providers such as Fillout
- From your interactions with our client portal or mobile app
- From our credit platform providers (Mercury, Trail, BrokerEngine)
- From our aggregator (Finsure) or lender partners
- From service providers you authorise, such as conveyancers or valuers

We do not currently use cookies, web beacons, analytics tools or ad pixels to collect tracking data.

## Why We Collect Your Information

We collect and use your information to:

- Provide mortgage broking and credit assistance
- Submit loan applications on your behalf
- Verify your identity (AML/CTF obligations)
- Provide access to our tools, calculators, or mobile app
- Communicate with you regarding your loan, updates, or reminders
- Comply with legal or regulatory obligations
- Support initiatives like FundEd (our donation-linked loan program)

We may also send you relevant updates, newsletters, or product offers. You can opt out at any time.

## Storage and Security

We store your personal and credit information securely through platforms including Microsoft 365, Google Drive, Salesforce, and Infynity. All systems are protected by secure logins and data encryption.

Where we use third-party service providers, we require those providers to handle your data securely and in line with applicable privacy standards. Some data may be stored on servers located outside Australia.

## Retention

We retain your information for a minimum of seven (7) years after the completion of our services, in accordance with our legal and regulatory obligations.

## Marketing and Communication

We may contact you about relevant services, refinancing opportunities, or industry updates via email or phone. You can opt out of direct marketing at any time by contacting us or using the unsubscribe link in our emails.

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with partners or service providers where relevant to your enquiry or loan strategy, with your consent.

## Disclosure of Personal and Credit Information

Your data may be shared with:

- Lenders and mortgage insurers to support your application
- Our aggregator and associated credit platforms
- Credit reporting bodies
- Service providers such as identity verification, valuation or document storage firms
- Government or regulatory agencies (e.g. ASIC, ATO) as required
- Referral partners (with your consent)
- Our school-donation partner FundEd if you choose to participate

Some data may be stored in cloud platforms accessible from overseas. While specific server locations may vary, all providers are contractually required to maintain appropriate safeguards.

## Access and Correction

You have the right to access and correct the information we hold about you. To request this, contact us by email or phone. We aim to respond within 30 days.

If we cannot provide access or correct your information, we will explain why in writing and let you know your options to escalate.
